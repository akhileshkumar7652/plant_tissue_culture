Cleanroom Design Philosophy

A plant biotechnology tissue culture laboratory requires strict physical separation of procedures to prevent microbial contamination. Materials flow in a strict unidirection: Washing → Media Prep → Autoclaving → Aseptic Inoculation → Incubation Culture Room → Hardening.

Positive Pressure Air Cascade
Inoculation Room +15 to +20 Pa Highest Overpressure
Culture Room +10 Pa Positive Pressure
Media Prep +5 Pa Intermediate
Washing / Autoclave -5 Pa / Exhaust Steam Evacuation
Essential Equipment Checklist
  • ✓ Laminar Air Flow: Class 100 / ISO 5 sterile velocity $0.45 \text{ m/s}$
  • ✓ Autoclave: Saturated steam $121^\circ\text{C}$ @ $15 \text{ psi}$ ($15\text{–}20\text{ min}$)
  • ✓ Incubation Racks: LED spectrum ($450\text{ nm} + 660\text{ nm}$) timer 16h/8h
  • ✓ Growth Chamber: Closed Phytotron ($25^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}$, $65\% \text{ RH}$)
  • ✓ Instrumentation: Digital balance, pH meter, magnetic hotplate, microscope

1

Cutaway Room Shell

Construct two adjacent back walls and a floor from 8mm foam-PVC (Forex) board ($70\text{ cm } \times 45\text{ cm } \times 30\text{ cm } H$). Leave the front and right sides open for an unobstructed isometric view. Cover the floor with glossy sky-blue vinyl to simulate cleanroom epoxy.

2

Benches & Equipment Props

Craft workbenches using 5mm balsa wood or acrylic with gray vinyl tops. Fabricate the Laminar Hood from 2mm clear acrylic sheets. Use miniature glass homeopathic dram vials ($10\text{ mm} \times 45\text{ mm}$) filled with yellow-tinted epoxy resin for culture bottles.

3

Triple-Mode LED Lighting

Install 3 micro-circuits: (a) 6000K white micro-LEDs on the ceiling grid; (b) 400nm UV-blue LEDs inside the Laminar cabinet; (c) Pink/magenta plant grow LED tape under the culture rack shelves. Connect to a 3-gang rocker switch box on the baseboard.
